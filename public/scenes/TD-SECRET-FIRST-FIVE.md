# Turbo Dismount — first five maps (CONTAIN)

Dawon asked for the first five "secret door" levels. Turbo Dismount's studio is **Secret Exit**; there is no separate secret-door pack in the original game wiki. These are the **first five standard maps** in IGN/fandom order.

## Scenes

### `td-01-original-classic` — TD1 Original Classic
Short hill, a wall in the path, then a jump. Does the dummy clear or pancake?

### `td-02-chicken` — TD2 Chicken
Straight road. Oncoming car. Who swerves — or who wins the smash?

### `td-03-aiming-high` — TD3 Aiming High
Dive the underpass while traffic sits overhead. Clear the mouth or eat the span.

### `td-04-chicken-twist` — TD4 Chicken Twist
Twisting road. Oncoming bus, van, car. Survive the weave or collect them all.

### `td-05-space-program` — TD5 Space Program
Long steep ramp toward the sky. Make the lip — then the ground collects the score.

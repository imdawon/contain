# What makes a video fun

Curiosity. Tension. Payoff. If one is missing, people leave.

## The promise is a question they cannot leave unanswered

The first thing they see has to make them need the answer. “Which car overtakes the truck and survives?” You cannot get it from the thumbnail. You have to watch. If they already know, they are gone.

## Cash it immediately

They never clicked. They are already moving, often with the sound off. The first frame has to stop the thumb and be the thing they came for. Then show there is more. Match the promise, then exceed it. If they came for a wagon down a hill and you give them a dummy standing still, they leave.

## Give them a game they can see

Fun is sport. They need to know the score without thinking. A number. A count. Who is left. They start predicting and rooting. The overlay is not decoration. It is how they feel each attempt.

## Pay them often

If the only payoff is the last frame, most people never get there. Stack mini-stories: try, almost, result. Then a harder try. Clean successes make the wreck mean something. All survive is a demo. All die is noise. The mix is the joke.

## Keep a question open

After two succeed, they have to see the next one. While one result lands, the next attempt is already lining up. One isolated bang is a light going out, not a game. A prize at the end is why they sit through a lull. If they can skip to the last frame and get the answer, they will. Show the ending coming, then make the middle change so a peek is not the same as watching.

## Kill every dull second

No wasted words. No still shot after it is over. No inspect panel. No talking about something they did not click for. Whatever you said you would do, end the second you do it.

## They have to watch it twice

Finishing once is not enough. Rewatches are how a short goes over 100%. A twist you only catch the second time, a loop that feeds the first frame. If it is under 30 seconds and nobody loops it, it dies.

## Cool wreck, not a sad one

Cartoon danger. Toy dummy, fire, ragdoll. Not a person getting hurt. They rewatch what looks like a game. They bounce off pain.

## Fun, in one line

You need to know what happens next, you can see the score, almost-success makes the crash hit, it pays off before you get bored, and you want to watch it again.

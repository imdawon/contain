# V1 wagon: what we should see

Dummy, wagon, hill, grenade. Nothing else.

You open on him already tied to the wagon and already going. The hill is obvious. The wagon is fast enough that you feel the drop in the first second, like a coaster, not a cart someone forgot to push. If you can count the boards, it is too slow.

The pin pulls late. Not at the top. After they are clearly racing, close to the lip, so the fuse is cooking through the fastest part of the ride. You can see it is live and still have to wait for the jump.

At the end of the ramp they launch. Wagon and dummy leave the surface together, still fast. They do not nosedive or tip over the lip like they ran out of hill. They go out into the air.

The bang is after they have left the ramp, while he is still up. Peak of the jump or just after, still in the air, not on the way down to the floor. He comes apart up there. That is the test: does the grenade get him before he lands.

If the pin is early, the bang is on the ramp. If the wagon is slow, the lip is a bump and he tips. The picture we want is speed, then air, then bang in the air.

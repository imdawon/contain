# Creative Content — short-form axioms

**Who:** Creative Content bot.
**Why it existed:** same job as the watch axioms, but for the one-second feed. If the first frame does not stop the thumb, nothing else matters. Used when inventing CONTAIN videos that have to work muted.

---

# What makes a short work

Same loop as any great video: curiosity, tension, payoff. The feed just gives you one second, then they swipe.

The people who actually win (Jenny Hoyos, MrBeast shorts, the physics clips that hold) say the same few things.

## The first frame is the whole ad

They never clicked. They are already moving. The first frame has to stop the thumb and make sense with the sound off. Visual. Simple. Three things in the shot, not twelve. If it could be a title and a thumbnail on a long video and still get a click, it is strong enough.

## Foreshadow the ending in the hook

A hook that only stops the scroll is half a hook. It also has to make the ending inevitable. Grandma opening a gift: you cannot leave until you see her face. Say what they will get. Then they stay to collect it.

## A mechanism they cannot skip

If they can jump to the last frame and get the answer, they will. The circle has to be closing. A count has to be going. Cars have to keep coming. Something in the middle must change so the end is different from a seek-bar peek.

## Pay off, then twist

Do the thing you promised. Then one extra beat they did not expect. Follow the expectation, then break it. That is why they watch it again.

## End the second you do it

Every leftover second is a percent of the video. A dull last second can drop retention off a cliff. No “let’s get started.” No victory lap. Cut on the hit.

## They have to watch it twice

A short that people finish once is not enough. Rewatches are how retention goes over 100%. Density, a joke that lands on the second pass, a loop that feeds the first frame, a twist you only catch again. If it is under 30 seconds and nobody loops it, it dies.

## Mute, then simple words

Most of them are in public with the sound off. The picture has to carry it. If you talk, talk like a fifth grader. MrBeast shorts sit nearer first grade. Big words make people work. They swipe instead.

## Fun, in one line

Stop the thumb, promise an ending they can see coming, make the middle unskippable, pay it, twist it, cut. Then make them watch it again.
